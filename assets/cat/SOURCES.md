own licences, not this repository's.

| `purr-1` … `purr-3` | Real cat purring sound (feedthestraycats, 354510) | Pixabay |

| `slap-3` | Angry wild cat roar (89) | Mixkit |
| `meow-1` | Cat meowing cute various (freesound_community, 26489) | Pixabay |
| `hello-1` … `hello-3` | Cat trilling (freesound_community, 75541) | Pixabay |

# The desktop cat

`rig/` is the skeleton, its animations and part pictures from the
"Cat game character pack" by GDS (bought under its commercial licence),
recoloured; `rig.json` is a compact export of its Spriter file.
